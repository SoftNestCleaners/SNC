Wheaton FAQ

Questions from Wheaton homeowners.

Can you safely clean an older wool or oriental-style rug?

In most cases, yes. We assess the fiber and dyes first and choose the gentlest effective method. If a rug is too fragile or valuable for in-home cleaning, we'll say so plainly.

My sofa is decades old but in good shape — worth cleaning?

Usually very much so. Older furniture was often built better than new. A careful deep clean can add years of life; we test a hidden area first to be sure the fabric agrees.

Do you serve all of Wheaton, including near the college and Cantigny?

Yes — downtown Wheaton, the college area, out by Cantigny and everywhere between. Wheaton is a short drive from our Naperville base.
