GOOD TO KNOW

Questions we get a lot.

How long does furniture take to dry?

Four to eight hours, depending on fabric, humidity and airflow.

Are the solutions safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We choose products with families in mind and say plainly if a stain needs something stronger.

Do you remove pet stains and odors?

In most cases. Success depends on the age of the stain and how deep it soaked.

Do I need to move furniture first?

Just clear small items and breakables. We work around larger pieces.
