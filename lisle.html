Local & Family-Run

The Arboretum Village's pollen problem.

They call Lisle the Arboretum Village for a reason. With the Morton Arboretum in the middle of town and a mature tree canopy over most neighborhoods, it's one of the greenest places in DuPage County. Beautiful — and hard on carpets. Pollen, leaf mold and fine dust ride in on shoes and paws all season long, and much of it ends up ground into carpet and upholstery.

We hear it from Lisle customers every spring and fall: someone in the house has allergies, and the vacuum alone isn't keeping up. Deep extraction pulls out what a household vacuum leaves behind — the fine allergens that settle below the surface of the pile.

Lisle FAQ

Questions from Lisle homeowners.

Does carpet cleaning actually help with allergies?

It helps with the reservoir. Carpets and upholstery hold pollen, dust and dander that vacuuming misses, and deep extraction removes a good share of it. It's not a medical fix, but allergy-prone households tell us they notice the difference.

What products do you use? We have an allergy-sensitive household.

Family- and pet-friendly solutions by default, rinsed thoroughly. If anyone in your home has a specific sensitivity, mention it when you book and we'll talk through exactly what we'd use.

Do you serve apartments and condos near Benedictine University?

Yes — houses, townhomes, apartments and condos across Lisle. Just let us know about stairs, elevators or parking when you book.
