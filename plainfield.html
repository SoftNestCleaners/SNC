On The Job

A typical Plainfield visit.

A typical Plainfield visit is a main floor and basement: living-room and hallway carpet, a well-used sofa, and an 8×10 rug from the great room. In older homes, add a pair of dining chairs or a headboard. Most visits wrap in two to three hours, and carpets are dry the same evening. Package jobs are the norm here — when a whole main floor gets done in one visit, most families add the basement stairs while we're set up.

Plainfield FAQ

Questions from Plainfield homeowners.

My home near downtown Plainfield is old — is that a problem?

Not at all. Older homes are normal for us. We check fabrics and fibers first, use gentler methods where needed, and flag anything we don't think should be cleaned.

Do you offer a better rate for cleaning several rooms at once?

Yes — three carpeted rooms or more booked together are quoted as a package. It usually beats the per-room math, and it's one visit instead of several.

Can you clean a finished basement?

Absolutely. Basements are some of our most common Plainfield jobs — carpet, sectional, and the area rug under the TV. We carry everything down with us.
