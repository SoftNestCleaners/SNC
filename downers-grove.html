A typical Downers Grove visit.

A typical Downers Grove visit is an entry-to-family-room job: hallway and stairs carpet, an entry rug that's taken a season of mud and road salt, and the sofa everyone lands on. Downers Grove FAQ

Questions from Downers Grove homeowners.

We live in an older Sears kit home — any special concerns?

Mostly good news: those homes were well built. For older original upholstery or rugs we test a hidden area and use gentler methods. If something shouldn't be wet-cleaned, we'll tell you before we touch it.

Can you get ground-in mud and salt out of entry rugs?

Usually yes — mud and road salt respond well to deep extraction. The sooner after the season, the better; stains left for years are less predictable, and we'll be honest about those.

Is there parking or access you need in Downers Grove's older neighborhoods?

Nothing special — a spot within reasonable hose distance of the door is plenty. Narrow parkways and shared driveways are normal for us; just mention anything unusual when you book.
