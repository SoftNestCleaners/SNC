Oswego FAQ

Questions from Oswego homeowners.

Is your cleaning safe for a crawling baby and a dog?

Yes. We choose kid- and pet-friendly products by default, and we'll tell you plainly if a particular stain needs anything stronger so you can decide.

Can you get old pet urine smell out of carpet?

Most of the time, yes. Success depends on the age of the stain and whether it reached the pad. We treat what we can reach and give you an honest read on the rest.

How far ahead should an Oswego appointment be booked?

A few days is usually enough. End-of-month move-in and move-out dates fill fastest, so book those as early as you can.
